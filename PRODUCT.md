# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese students (ages 16–25) preparing for IELTS. They study on phones and laptops, often between classes or in the evening. Their primary job on any given session: find an assignment for a specific skill, complete it, see the score. Secondary jobs: review past submissions, track improvement. They chose to be here — motivated but stressed about the exam.

## Product Purpose

Idest is an AI-powered IELTS practice platform covering all four skills (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking) with immediate AI-scored feedback. It replaces expensive tutoring and lifeless test-prep sites. Success looks like a student finishing a session feeling like they improved, not like they filled out a government form.

## Brand Personality

Alive · Playful · Sharp — warm orange energy and an orange cat mascot with genuine personality, but never distracting from actual studying. Confident, not corporate. Direct, not apologetic. The right mental model: a sharp friend who takes IELTS seriously and occasionally makes you smile.

## Anti-references

- **Generic IELTS prep sites** (British Council, IDP, IELTS.org): gray, institutional, flat, zero personality. Looks like a visa application form.
- **SaaS dashboard templates** (Shadcn defaults, Vercel-style admin panels, metric grids, sidebar navs): cold monochrome, corporate data density, wrong register entirely.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice feels like progress.** Every interaction should leave the student sensing forward motion, not bureaucratic friction.
2. **Warmth without noise.** The orange palette and cat mascot create personality; they never compete with the content being studied.
3. **Focus when it matters.** Test-taking surfaces strip everything to just the question. Distraction is an enemy.
4. **Sharp, not soft.** Alive and playful does not mean round, pastel, or childish. Sharp typography, confident sizing, no padding for padding's sake.
5. **Vietnamese first.** Copy, hierarchy, and flow are designed for Vietnamese readers, not translated from English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Vietnamese text with full diacritics renders correctly (Oswald 700 for display, Plus Jakarta Sans 400-800 for body, both Vietnamese-capable). Reduced motion respected via prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard-navigable test-taking flow (Tab to next question, Space to select).
